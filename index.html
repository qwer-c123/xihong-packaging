6-Step High Precision OEM/ODM Process

From initial 3D concept rendering and rapid 3D prototyping to high-speed automated stamping and export pallet loading.

01

3D Design & Die-Line Creation

Our structural engineers provide exact 2D die-line templates within 24 hours. Free 3D photorealistic digital proofing to verify artwork scaling before tooling.

02

In-House Tooling & Mold Shop

Over 3,500 standard molds in stock. For custom bespoke shapes, our CNC mold center manufactures precision stamping dies in just 10-14 days.

03

FUJI Offset Printing & Coating

Equipped with state-of-the-art FUJI PRIMEX 4-color offset printing machines. Supports CMYK, Pantone spot colors, matte/glossy varnishes, and crackle finishes.

04

Automated Stamping & Assembly

40 high-speed stamping lines (30 semi-auto, 10 fully automatic lines) with daily production exceeding 80,000 units, guaranteeing on-time bulk shipments.

05

100% Quality Inspection (AQL 2.5)

40+ dedicated QC staff inspect every piece for scratch resistance, embossing alignment, air-tightness, and coating adhesion prior to packing.

06

Export Palletizing & Global DDP Delivery

Export standard 5-ply corrugated master cartons with plastic polybags and corner protector pallets. Direct DDP, FOB, CIF sea/air freight to 80+ countries.
